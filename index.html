Senior Capstone Project

Plantify AR

Unity • C# • SQLite • Augmented Reality

Plantify is an augmented reality app that I worked on with a team for my senior capstone project at UNT.

The goal of the app is to make learning about plants more interactive and interesting for kids. Users can explore different plants in augmented reality or in a regular 3D view.

They can also select different parts of a plant, like the roots, leaves, or flowers, to learn more about them.

We built the app using Unity, C#, and SQLite. It was a good opportunity to get experience working with a team on a larger project and putting what I've learned into practice.
